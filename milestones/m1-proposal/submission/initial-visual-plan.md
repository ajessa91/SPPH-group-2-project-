# M1: Initial Visual Plan

## Planned Figure

The initial visualization will be a bar chart comparing hospital beds per 10,000 people across selected countries using the WHO Global Health Observatory dataset.

The x-axis will show the countries, and the y-axis will show the number of hospital beds per 10,000 population.

We will use the most recent available observation for each selected country and clearly label the reporting year.

## Audience Purpose

The figure will help public health students, policymakers, and healthcare planners understand differences in hospital bed availability across countries.

It may encourage further discussion about healthcare infrastructure and where additional information is needed to understand differences in resources.

## Risk of Misinterpretation

One risk is that viewers may assume countries with more hospital beds have better healthcare systems.

This conclusion would be misleading because the number of hospital beds does not account for healthcare quality, staffing, accessibility, or other healthcare resources.

Another risk is that the data may come from different reporting years, making comparisons less directly comparable.

To reduce these risks, the figure will clearly identify the reporting years and include a caption explaining that hospital bed availability is only one measure of healthcare resources.

## Future Visualization Direction

If the dataset contains enough observations across multiple years, I also plan to create a line chart showing changes in hospital bed availability over time for selected countries.

This would help the audience understand whether the reported availability of hospital beds has increased, decreased, or remained relatively stable.
