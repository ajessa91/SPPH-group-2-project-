# M1: Project Proposal

## Project and Group

**Project Title:** Global Differences in Hospital Bed Availability

**Group Members:** Alyssa Jessa (working independently while awaiting group reassignment, as previous group members withdrew from the course).

## Audience and KT Purpose

The intended audience is public health students, healthcare planners, and policymakers interested in understanding differences in healthcare resources between countries.

The purpose of this project is to communicate patterns in hospital bed availability using publicly available data from the World Health Organization (WHO). This analysis may help the audience identify differences in hospital infrastructure and consider where further research or planning may be needed.

Hospital bed availability alone does not determine healthcare quality or accessibility, so the results will be presented as descriptive comparisons rather than conclusions about the overall performance of a country's healthcare system.

## Research Questions

**Primary Question:** How does the number of hospital beds per 10,000 people differ across selected countries based on available WHO data?

**Secondary Question:** How has hospital bed availability changed over time in selected countries with multiple years of reported data?

## Expected Data Source

The project will use the World Health Organization (WHO) Global Health Observatory indicator "Hospital beds (per 10,000 population)."

Source:
https://www.who.int/data/gho/data/indicators/indicator-details/GHO/hospital-beds-%28per-10-000-population%29

The dataset contains country-level hospital bed availability estimates. Reporting years and data availability vary across countries.

The data will be accessed as a CSV file and analyzed using R.

## Planned Analytic Output

The project will produce:

- A summary table showing hospital bed availability for selected countries.
- A bar chart comparing hospital beds per 10,000 people across countries.
- A line chart showing changes in hospital bed availability over time for countries with sufficient observations.
- A short Quarto report explaining the findings and limitations.
- A simple Quarto dashboard allowing readers to explore country-level comparisons.

## Known Limitations

- Data availability and reporting years differ between countries.
- Healthcare reporting methods and definitions may vary between countries.
- Hospital bed availability does not measure quality of care, healthcare staffing, or accessibility.
- Direct comparisons may be misleading if countries have observations from different years.
- This is an observational, descriptive project and cannot establish causation.
- Missing values may limit which countries can be included in comparisons.
